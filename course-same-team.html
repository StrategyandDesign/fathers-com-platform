Same Team

BUILT FOR FATHERS RAISING CHILDREN ACROSS TWO HOMES

Twelve short films on co-parenting as one team for your children: cool the heat, keep messages businesslike, and hand off with a calm ritual.

Self-paced. About twelve minutes each. A Certified Facilitator is available when your seat is claimed.

Preview is practice only. Earn proof through a Certified Organization.

Important notes

Completion never requires contact with your child. No practice asks you to go beyond the rules that govern you now or any court order.

SESSION 1~12 MIN

One Team for the Children

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Whatever we are to each other, we are one team for the child.”

One scene. A parent-teacher meeting. The two adults sit one seat apart, not touching, not fighting, and both sign the same plan for the same child. The child sleeps easier that night.

In the room. What being on one team means when the adults may not be together, with the child's experience at the center.

What you leave with. You can state the shared frame in child-centered terms.

THE PRACTICE

Write one child-centered team sentence and keep it where you will see it before hard contact.

The science in the room: A child caught between adults carries the load in his body; one shared frame lowers it.

SESSION 2~12 MIN

Write the Shared Goal

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“One child-centered sentence you can both hold.”

One scene. He wants a ten-point manifesto. He writes one sentence instead: We keep school nights calm so she can sleep and learn. That sentence steers the next hard text.

In the room. You draft one child-centered goal sentence clear enough to hold when heat rises. Agreement on everything is not required.

What you leave with. You have one child-centered sentence you can both hold.

THE PRACTICE

Write one shared-goal sentence. Send it through a permitted channel, or file it to send when allowed.

The science in the room: One clear goal steers hard moments better than a stack of demands.

SESSION 3~12 MIN

Flooded Means Pause

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Flooded means pause. Twenty minutes, then resume.”

One scene. The message thread heats up and he can feel his heartbeat in his ears. He names the pause, puts the phone face down, and walks. Later he comes back, and the conversation works.

In the room. When your heart is racing, the thinking brain goes offline. You train recognizing flood and pausing about twenty minutes before you resume.

What you leave with. You can recognize flooding in yourself and pause before you make it worse.

THE PRACTICE

Rehearse the pause rule once. Use it on any heated exchange this week and log both ends.

The science in the room: Past a racing heartbeat nobody absorbs a word; the body needs about twenty minutes to come down.

SESSION 4~12 MIN

Break Clean

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Break off clean. Name when you will resume.”

One scene. He almost ghosts the thread. Instead he writes: I am flooded. Let's resume at 8 tonight. At eight he shows up. The topic survives because the break was clean.

In the room. You practice a short break-off script that names the pause and the resume time. No speech. No slam.

What you leave with. You can break off clean and resume when you said.

THE PRACTICE

Write a clean break-off line with a resume time. Use it once on a heated call or thread.

The science in the room: Naming the resume turns a pause into a trustworthy reset.

SESSION 5~12 MIN

Businesslike

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Short, factual, about the child.”

One scene. Three lines. Pickup Friday at five. Homework due Sunday. Doctor confirmed Tuesday. Nothing about the past. The reply comes back the same way.

In the room. Brief, factual, child-focused communication. Where the adults are not together, the low-contact model that keeps heat out of the channel.

What you leave with. You can send a businesslike message that carries only what the child needs.

THE PRACTICE

Draft three businesslike messages. Send whichever a permitted channel allows; file the rest.

The science in the room: Less exposure to adult conflict is a direct gift to a child's nervous system.

SESSION 6~12 MIN

Cooler Channels

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Use the channel that lowers heat.”

One scene. The text thread is a furnace. He moves the logistics to a written log and saves voice for a scheduled call after a pause. The facts still move. The heat drops.

In the room. You choose channels on purpose. Some media raise heat before a word is read. Cooler channels protect the child from spillover.

What you leave with. You can pick the channel that lowers heat for logistics and hard topics.

THE PRACTICE

Name your hottest channel and your cooler backup. Move one real logistics item to the cooler channel this week.

The science in the room: Medium matters; channel choice can prevent a fight before content does.

SESSION 7~12 MIN

Reliability Buys Trust

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Trust is bought with reliability.”

One scene. For a month he is never once late for the agreed call, never once asks for an exception. Nobody says anything. The tone in the messages changes anyway.

In the room. How a parent earns caregiving trust through small kept promises over time. Encouragement follows the track record.

What you leave with. You can earn trust in steps with small reliability.

THE PRACTICE

Identify one small reliable act and do it consistently for a week. Log it.

The science in the room: Reliability, repeated, is what reopens the gate.

SESSION 8~12 MIN

Never Against an Order

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Never ask a child to carry an adult fight, and never against an order.”

One scene. He wants an exception. He wants the child to pass a message. He does neither. He keeps the written plan exactly. Trust grows inside the line, not against it.

In the room. Court orders and agreements are the floor. Trust is earned inside them, never against them. Children are never messengers for adult fights.

What you leave with. You can operate inside any legal boundary and keep adult conflict off the child.

THE PRACTICE

List the rules that govern you now. Choose one small act of compliance you will keep perfectly this week. Never use the child as a messenger.

The science in the room: Trust built against an order is not trust; it is risk the child pays for.

SESSION 9~12 MIN

What the Child Carries

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“The child carries the distance. Lighten the load.”

One scene. Every week the child leaves one home with a bag. He learns what is hard about the bag and fixes his end: the charger, the book, the jacket for the colder house.

In the room. Your child's experience of moving between two homes, the load at the transition, and the warmth that builds resilience regardless of the arrangement.

What you leave with. You can see the transition through the child's eyes and lighten one concrete load.

THE PRACTICE

Ask one open question about moving between homes if contact allows, or file it. Fix one logistics friction on your side.

The science in the room: Transitions are stress points; warm, practical help lightens what the child carries.

SESSION 10~12 MIN

Two Homes, One Child

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Predictable rules beat perfect agreement.”

One scene. The houses will never match perfectly. He stops fighting for identical rules and makes his own home predictable: bedtime, homework, phone off at dinner. The child stops guessing.

In the room. Perfect agreement is rare. Predictable rules at your place give the child ground to stand on even when the homes differ.

What you leave with. You can run a few clear, predictable rules the child can count on at your place.

THE PRACTICE

Write three predictable rules for your parenting time. Post them where you will follow them.

The science in the room: A child can adapt to two homes when each home is steady.

SESSION 11~12 MIN

The Handoff

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Predictable beats perfect.”

One scene. Same spot, same time, a calm two-minute handoff. Within a month the child stops going quiet on the ride there. Nothing was said. The routine said it.

In the room. A predictable handoff carries the child's things, the practical facts, and a calm goodbye. Old disputes stay off the curb.

What you leave with. You can run a predictable handoff that keeps adult heat away from the child.

THE PRACTICE

Write your handoff checklist: place, time, items, facts, goodbye. Rehearse once.

The science in the room: The handoff is where the child feels the team most directly.

SESSION 12~12 MIN

The Handoff Ritual

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Same place, same time, same calm script.”

One scene. Bag, jacket, homework. See you Thursday at five. Same script every week. The child starts handing over the bag before anyone asks.

In the room. You lock a short ritual: same place, same time, same calm script. You leave with a one-page plan and a first checkpoint.

What you leave with. You leave with a repeatable handoff ritual and a first checkpoint.

THE PRACTICE

Complete a one-page handoff ritual plan and walk it through with a cohort partner or facilitator. After return, execute one handoff to the plan and log it.

The science in the room: A predictable ritual says what words cannot at the transition.
