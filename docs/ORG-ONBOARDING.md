2. **Mint join code.** Copy `/profile.html?join=CODE`.
3. Person must have signed in once (a `profiles` row).
4. **Grant both** `org_admin` and `circle_leader` with that org_id (Grant both button, or People form with the both checkbox).
5. They open org.html (join link) and lead.html (claims, flags, mailto).

Claims gate **certificates**, not films or the Profile. A father trains without a seat claim. The facilitator stays available.
