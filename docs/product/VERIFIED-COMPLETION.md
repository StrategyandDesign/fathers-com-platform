# Verified-only completion (locked)

Product rule as of August 2026. Optimized for organizations that pay for certification.

## What any man can do free
- Keystone Profile
- Twelve-week plan
- Course **preview** (try the player flow; not proof)

## What only a claimed seat can do
- Official enrollment in a certificate course
- Facilitator review of the final
- Certificate of Completion
- Public serial / verify page

## Hard boundaries
- Preview never mints a serial
- Preview never says the man "completed" the certificate for proof purposes
- A private or preview sitting cannot be upgraded into a serial later; verified work happens under a claimed seat
- Organizations sell accountability and checkable proof, not exclusive access to the film ideas

## Participant line
Train free. Earn proof through a Certified Organization.

## Organization line
Men can start on their own. Only your claimed seats mint certificates a funder can check.
