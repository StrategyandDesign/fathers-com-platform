# Father surfaces vs Desk surfaces

Canonical doctrine: POSITIONING.md §24 (Education-first, agentic underneath).

## Father-facing (education-first)
- Home, Profile, Plan, course player, certificate
- Language: learn, train, complete
- One clear next step
- No agent chrome unless it feels like a coach helping him finish

## Desk / org-facing (agentic)
- Facilitator Desk, claims, cohorts, efficacy, sponsor ops
- Language: who needs outreach, who is stuck, what proof shipped
- Agents earn their place by raising completion and reducing facilitator load

## Test before shipping any agent feature
1. Does a father feel more able to finish this week?
2. Or does a facilitator/org remove friction so more fathers finish?
3. If neither, do not ship it on a participant surface.
