# Taking part in the Keystone pilot

**Read this before you decide. Ask any question you want. Take it home if you like.**

## What this is

Fathers.com is testing a fatherhood assessment called the Keystone Profile.
You would answer questions about yourself as a father, get a report back, and
follow a plan for about 90 days.

## What you would do

- Answer 128 questions. Most men finish in 25 to 35 minutes.
- Read your report.
- Work the plan at your own pace.
- Answer the questions once more at the end.

## What we get, and what we do not

We get your answers. That is all.

**We do not get your name.** We do not get your email address, your birth date,
your case number, or your address. Returning Home gives you a code and a PIN.
You sign in with those. To us you are a code and nothing else.

Returning Home keeps the list of which code belongs to which man. We never see
that list and we never ask for it.

## Your participation is voluntary

**Saying yes, saying no, or stopping partway has no bearing on your parole,
your probation, your standing in this program, your release date, or any other
decision about your custody or supervision. Nobody who makes those decisions
will be told whether you took part.**

You can stop at any time. You do not have to give a reason. Nothing happens to
you if you do.

## Who sees your answers

Your report is yours. Returning Home staff do not see your individual answers
or your individual report unless you choose to show them.

We report to funders in groups only, never one man at a time, and never in a
group small enough that someone could work out who you are.

## How long we keep it

We keep your answers for the length of the study and one year after.
On or before **[DATE]**, Returning Home destroys the list linking codes to
names. After that day nobody can connect your answers back to you, including us.

## Risks

Some questions ask about your children, your past, and how you see yourself as
a father. A few men find that hard to sit with. You can skip any question or
stop entirely.

There is no payment for taking part.
