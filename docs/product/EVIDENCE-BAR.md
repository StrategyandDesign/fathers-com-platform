# The evidence bar: from unrated to rated

Both incumbent fatherhood curricula are rated Does Not Currently Meet
Criteria on the Title IV-E Prevention Services Clearinghouse (last reviewed
July 2025): one because no studies met eligibility, the other because no
study achieved a moderate or high rating on design and execution. Under the
Family First Prevention Services Act, a Clearinghouse rating is what makes a
program eligible for Title IV-E reimbursement. As of June 2026 the
Clearinghouse had reviewed 222 programs and rated 102 promising or better.
No fatherhood curriculum holds that ground. This document is the path to it.

## What a rating requires
1. An eligible study: a comparison-group design (randomized or rigorous
   quasi-experimental), published or publicly available.
2. Moderate or high design and execution: adequate baseline equivalence,
   acceptable attrition, valid outcome measures.
3. A favorable, sustained effect on an eligible outcome domain.

## The sequence
1. Clear the two internal blockers first: replace demo scoring; resolve the
   norms number with Dr. Canfield (POSITIONING.md 8). No outcome claim of
   any kind before both clear.
2. Instrument the pipeline that already exists: baseline at intake, exit
   retake, cohort rollup. Align measure definitions with nFORM so FORGE
   grantee partners report once, not twice.
3. Run cohorts and accumulate clean pre-post data. Pre-post alone earns no
   rating; it earns partners and a fundable study design.
4. Commission the comparison-group study with an external evaluator.
   Second Chance Act solicitations already give priority to independently
   evaluated approaches; the evaluation itself is fundable.
5. Submit for Clearinghouse review. A Promising rating or better unlocks
   the child welfare channel neither incumbent can enter today.

## The claims rules, in force now
1. No page prints "evidence-based" until a rating exists.
2. The Efficacy Report is "built for the evidence bar," never proof earned.
3. Printed norming language is magnitude only until section 8 resolves.
tools/check_release.py enforces all three on every release.
