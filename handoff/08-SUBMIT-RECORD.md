# Submit record (even numbers)

Erik tracks official copies by **even submit number** and **date**, not by “whatever is on `review` today.”

- **`review`** — moving line. Safe to pull for the latest draft.
- **`submit/2`, `submit/4`, …** — frozen. A submit never moves after it is cut.
- **Issue titled `Submit N — date`** — the human record and notification.

The table lives in `/SUBMITS.md` at the repo root.

Submit **2** (19 Aug 2026) is the first official stamp. The 18 Aug share had no number.
