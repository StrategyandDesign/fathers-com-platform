Session outlines (1–7)
SESSION 1one session

First Secret: Commitment

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Present physically, emotionally, spiritually; commitment builds trust.”

One scene. He chooses to be present in the room, in the conversation, and in the spiritual lead of the home.

In the room. Commitment as physical, emotional, and spiritual presence; trust as the fruit.

What you leave with. You treat presence as a commitment your child can feel across all three dimensions.

THE PRACTICE

Name one physical, one emotional, and one spiritual way you will show up this week.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Commitment builds trust and lays the foundation for lasting influence.

SESSION 2one session

Second Secret: Knowing Your Child

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Unique personality, needs, and interests open deeper connection.”

One scene. He names what makes this child different and asks a question that fits that child, not a script.

In the room. Personality, needs, and interests as the path to meaningful connection.

What you leave with. You can name concrete traits, needs, and interests for each child.

THE PRACTICE

Write a one-page profile of who this child is now (personality, fears, current passions, next six months), with the mother or caregiver if that channel is safe. No visit required.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Understanding uniqueness opens the door to deeper connection.

SESSION 3one session

Third Secret: Showing Up Consistently

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Stability through consistent actions, values, and discipline.”

One scene. Week after week he keeps the same actions and values so the home feels steady.

In the room. Stability via consistent actions, values, and discipline.

What you leave with. You treat consistency as security your child can feel.

THE PRACTICE

Pick one action, one value, and one discipline habit you will keep the same this week.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Children thrive on stability; consistency builds confidence.

SESSION 4one session

Fourth Secret: Protecting and Providing Security

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Physical, emotional, and spiritual safety and provision.”

One scene. He meets a need and removes a threat so the child feels safe and valued.

In the room. Safety and provision across physical, emotional, and spiritual dimensions.

What you leave with. You can name how you protect and provide in each of the three dimensions.

THE PRACTICE

Name one protective move and one provision move you will make this week.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Protection and provision communicate value to the child.

SESSION 5one session

Fifth Secret: Loving Their Mother

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Honor their mother. Civil if you are not together. Never undercut her.”

One scene. On an ordinary day he almost talks their mother down within earshot. He stops. The child will not hear him undercut her, even if the adults are not a couple.

In the room. How he speaks about their mother. Honor her. Civil if you are not together. Never undercut her. Not a couple-therapy session.

What you leave with. You have one sentence you will not say about their mother, and a civil child-only note if the coparent channel is safe.

THE PRACTICE

Write one sentence you will not say about their mother this week. If a coparent channel is safe, send one short civil note about the child. If it is not, keep the sentence in your notes.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Children should not have to choose a side or hear you tear her down.

SESSION 6one session

Sixth Secret: Active Listening

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Two-way. Full attention. Not an interrogation.”

One scene. On an ordinary day he puts the phone face down. One real conversation with a program peer, a coparent, or a call he already has. After, he writes what they actually said, not what he planned to say.

In the room. Active listening is two-way, with full attention, not an interrogation. The usual rival is the phone.

What you leave with. You can put the phone face down, hear them out, and say back what you heard.

THE PRACTICE

Put the phone face down for one real conversation this week (program peer, coparent, a call you already have). After, write what they actually said, not what you planned to say.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Full attention is the skill. Advice can wait.

SESSION 7one session

Seventh Secret: Spiritual Equipping

SHAPE PREVIEW · PLACEHOLDER

Final film goes here. Until then this shape still holds the 16:9 slot.

Open in the player

“Live convictions a child can trust. Faith is welcome. This is not a chapel course.”

One scene. He names one conviction and lives it in the open at work, in the program, or at home. If faith is his, he lives it. If it is not, he keeps his word anyway. No chapel track.

In the room. Spiritual equipping here means grounded convictions you live, with room for faith. Fathers come with faith and without. Kids watch what you live. This is not a chapel course.

What you leave with. You leave with one conviction you will live in the open this week.

THE PRACTICE

Name one conviction you will live in the open this week at work, in the program, or at home. If faith is yours, live it. If it is not, keep your word anyway.

Complete this in the player after you pass the checkpoint. That is when the week counts.

The science in the room: Kids watch what you live. This is not a required chapel track.
