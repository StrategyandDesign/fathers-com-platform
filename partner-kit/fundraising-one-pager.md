# Fathers.com — founding gift one-pager

For donors and foundations. Not a public page. Not participant copy.
Sources are 2018–2026 only. Claims follow docs/product/POSITIONING.md.

---

**The ask.** A working year-one founding gift of **$260,000** pays for the films, the first credentials, and the measurement spine. The man still pays nothing.

**The inversion.** National Center for Fathering certifies organizations and Leaders. Fathers complete free. A Certificate of Completion is serialed, signed, and checkable. Sponsorship pays the credential, never a seat.

**The market that already has money.**

- ACF is in a five-year HMRF cycle (2025–2030): 109 awards in 38 states, **$150 million a year**, including **50 FORGE Fatherhood** grantees. Those grants already buy parenting skill, staff training, and nFORM reporting.
- Programs already buy kits. The incumbent 24:7 Dad facilitator kit publishes at **$899**. Facilitator training there is optional. Nobody in this category runs a renewable, revocable registry.
- Opioid settlements obligated to states and localities exceed **$50 billion**. Family and parenting work is an approved recovery-support use in multiple state MOUs.
- The Second Chance Act has made **148** awards aimed at incarcerated parents and their families. Independent evaluation is a priority on those solicitations.

**What the last eight years actually show.**

- Father engagement is still tied to children’s cognitive, social, and emotional development in reviews published 2019–2024.
- Fatherhood programs move involvement, parenting, and co-parenting a little. Co-parenting is the largest effect. They do not reliably move jobs or child support. The binding constraint is recruitment, retention, and what is taught — not a cheaper binder.
- ACF’s 2024 core-components analysis: parenting knowledge, the father’s role, and an individual format enlarge those effects. That is Film → Check-in → Action, with a Leader who can see who stopped.
- Residential programs can put fathering in the house. In a 2019 randomized pilot, both fatherhood interventions were welcomed on the unit. Men’s treatment still rarely includes parenting.
- After deployment, Adaptive Parenting Tools trials (2020–2021) show military families will take a parenting program when it is skill. Fathers with more emotion-regulation difficulty gained more. This is not trauma treatment. The military surface stays dark until rehab cohorts prove completion.
- Title IV-E Prevention Services Clearinghouse, July 2025: **24:7 Dad** and **InsideOut Dad** do not currently meet criteria. No fatherhood curriculum holds a Promising-or-better rating. That channel is open. We will not print a rating we have not earned.

**What we will not say.** This product is not rated. The Efficacy Report is built for the evidence bar. It is not proof already earned. We do not sell relapse reduction, a clinical instrument, or a public military brand.

**The door.** Rehab and residential programs first. Community FORGE grantees next (they already have the budget line). Armed-forces units later, after completion evidence, through a chaplain or veteran peer — not a new vertical.
