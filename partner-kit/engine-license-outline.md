# Engine license outline

STATUS: DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW. Not for execution. This is the governance
skeleton for licensing the certification engine to an outside subject-matter
authority. Counsel finalizes parties, term, fees, and jurisdiction.

## The three invariants, non-negotiable in every license
1. The participant document is always a Certificate of Completion. No
   license may issue a document that implies clinical treatment, licensure,
   or regulatory approval.
2. Verification is always public, and revocation is always shown. No
   private registries, no scrubbed history.
3. The education line always holds (docs/product/POSITIONING.md section 16). The
   licensee never diagnoses, screens, treats, or counsels under the
   platform, and never uses those words about the program.

## What the platform entity grants
1. The engine: instruments, courses, claims, enrollment, certificates,
   serials under the licensee's assigned prefix, public verification,
   revocation, cohort rollups, and Efficacy Reports.
2. The certification standard: organization and facilitator certification
   under the registrar's process, including the supervised first cohort.
3. The licensee's entry surface on the platform.

## What the licensee owns
1. Its curriculum and its customization, inside the invariants.
2. Its instrument's validity: who normed it, on whom, and the published
   statement. The platform prints the statement; the authority stands
   behind it.
3. Its signing authority: the name and title on its certificates.

## What the platform entity retains
1. The registrar function and revocation authority across every vertical.
2. The serial namespace registry; prefixes are assigned, unique, permanent.
3. The right to suspend a vertical that breaks an invariant, with the
   suspension visible on verification, the same standard organizations and
   facilitators live under.

## Counsel to determine
Term, fees, exclusivity by field, data ownership on participant accounts
(platform doctrine: the account belongs to the man), insurance, indemnity,
termination and wind-down with certificates remaining verifiable forever.
